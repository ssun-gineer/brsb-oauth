BRSB Local Runtime

A private, non-commercial research environment management tool.

About

BRSB Local Runtime supports the management, transfer, verification, and recovery of files in a personal research and development environment.

This tool is operated by a single individual and is not offered as a public service.

Google Drive Integration

The tool uses Google OAuth 2.0 authorization to perform file operations within the permissions granted by its operator.

Google Drive access is limited to the drive.file permission scope.
